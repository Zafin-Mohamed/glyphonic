Glyphonic

Sync your Nothing Phone (4a) Glyph Bar to any ringtone or notification tone. Free. Open source. Runs entirely in your browser.

01 — Load audio

.ogg files only (Opus or Vorbis) — we edit the light data, not the audio
00:00.00 / 00:00.00

02 — Compose

Click a zone lane to place a light hit at that moment. Click a hit to remove it. Drag its edge to resize.

03 — Export

Generates a new .ogg with the Glyph light data embedded. Set it as your ringtone or notification sound from your phone's normal sound settings.

Before you use this

This project reverse-engineers a documented but unofficial file format and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nothing Technology Limited. It has not been verified end-to-end against every phone or OS version. Test it on your own device before relying on it, and expect the first few exports to need debugging. See the technical spec this is built on for details.

Glyphonic is free, open source, and runs entirely client-side — your audio never leaves your browser. Not affiliated with Nothing Technology Limited.
